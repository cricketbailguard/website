Manufacturers

The design is published under CC BY 4.0 as prior art, which means you are free to manufacture and sell bail guards commercially. You do not need our permission and you owe us nothing. We would simply like to know, so we can point clubs and leagues towards you.

Get in touch

If you run a league, coach a team, or want to manufacture these, we would like to hear from you. Tell us roughly how many sets you need and where you are, and we will help.
