Cricket bails shouldn't become projectiles.

A bail guard is a simple tether that lets the bails lift normally, then stops them becoming projectiles. It retrofits any wicket, costs very little, and the design is published free for anyone to use.

Where the data comes from

This is a lower bound — deliberately

These counts come only from completed dismissals on the scorecard. They do not capture the many other times the bails are dislodged:

  • Run-out and stumping attempts where the batter was not out
  • A bowled or hit-wicket off a no-ball
  • Any other ball that hits the stumps without a wicket being given

The real number is therefore higher. We count what we can verify from official records, and we do not claim the device prevents a given injury — only how often it would be engaged.
