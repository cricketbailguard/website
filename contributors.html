The people behind this

A safety idea only becomes real when umpires, players, clubs and leagues are willing to try it in a live game. Everyone below helped move it from a drawing to a wicket.

Leagues and clubs

Dallas Cricket LeagueThe world’s first league to officially adopt bail guards, for its Fall 2026 season. 201 teams, more than 6,900 players. Volunteers led by Mr Kuljit Singh Nijjar worked tirelessly to make it happen at that scale: studying the design, putting out flyers, answering questions from thousands of players, packaging and distributing the guards across several distribution days alongside the tournament balls and bails, and staying on call through the opening games.Publicly backed
Grand Prairie Cricket ClubLed by Mr Faisal Akthar. Fitted bail guards on both Grand Prairie grounds and ran the GPCC Winter Cup with them on every wicket. GPCC volunteers also filmed every ball, from several angles and in excellent quality. That coverage is how much of the cricket community first saw the device work in real matches, and it was sharp enough for us to diagnose a cracked collar from the replay.Used in games
All Stars Frisco Cricket AcademyVolunteers, led by Mr Adi Kattari, made the devices used in the GPCC Winter Cup and some of the USA Hub Spring 2026 matches involving All Stars teams. Early support, and some of the very first trials.Early trials
USA Hub, DallasAdopting bail guards for the 2026 fall league.Adopting
